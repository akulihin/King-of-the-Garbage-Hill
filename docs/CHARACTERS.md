## TheBoys (Пацаны) — Tier 3, Int 3 / Str 5 / Speed 6 / Psyche 2

State classes `FrancieClass`, `ButcherClass`, `KimikoClass`, and `MMClass` live in `Game/Characters/TheBoys.cs`; `ButcherClass` also records whether the fourth team upgrade was resolved, the active combination and whether Butcher left. Permanent sups = Сайтама, Кратос, Загадочный Спартанец в маске, Кира, Homelander, Omni-man, Молодой Глеб, and old Глеб only during his Претендент round (`TheBoys.IsPermanentSup`); two more rotate. Per-player marks: `TheBoysSupMark`, `TheBoysVirus(+Source)`, `TheBoysMoralBlockedByMM`, plus the permanent `PassiveAbilitiesDisabledByKimiko` marker. UI serials distinguish member reveals, ultimate unlocks and lower-intensity combination unlocks.

- **Roster pull** (hidden, roll-time) — every canonical roster Super—Сайтама, Кратос, Загадочный Спартанец в маске, Кира, Homelander or Omni-man—independently doubles TheBoys' weight for subsequent natural rolls and all draft alternatives. Homelander additionally applies his separate ×6 (`+500%`) pull after that doubling, so Homelander alone changes the Tier-3 baseline `80 → 960`; Сайтама + Omni-man changes it `80 → 320`. Guaranteed reservations and forced line-ups count from the start because their roster is already fixed; a randomly rolled Super starts contributing only to later seats. Dynamic Молодой/Претендент Глеб classifications happen after roster generation and therefore cannot affect this roll (`StartGameLogic.GetTheBoysRosterRollMultiplier`/`HandleCharacterRoll`/`RollDraftOptions`; M186).
- **Team upgrade** — there is no separate `Пацаны` passive card. The four always-visible member cards are Francie, Butcher, Kimiko and M.M. A level-up gives **+2** to the chosen stat and levels the matching member: Int→Francie, Str→Butcher, Speed→Kimiko, Psyche→M.M. The first member upgrade appends the supplied improvement text; x4 of one member unlocks its ultimate. Exactly after the fourth distributed team upgrade, `ResolveTheBoysFourthUpgrade` checks one secret combination and never re-evaluates later distributions (`GameReactions.GetLvlUp` / `ResolveTheBoysFourthUpgrade`).
- **Francie** — first order at game start, replacements on rounds 4 and 7, 3-turn windows. At every draw a Most-wanted Rick still in the queue is moved to its front, so he is always the first order (`CP:408-411`; `CharacterPassives` Francie next-round case). Completion buffers **+1 regular point** in the winning round; an unresolved order expires at the end of rounds 3/6/9 and buffers **−1 regular point** before that same round settles, so both use the correct current-round multiplier. A final-turn win clears `OrderTarget` in the per-fight hook before expiry. Хим.оружие (level ≥1) still pays `chemLevel × (1 + enemy TooGood + enemy TooStronk)` **bonus** points on an eligible win (`CharacterPassives` Francie start/after-fight/end-of-round/next-round cases; M191; m10 fixed).
- **Butcher** — marks begin on round 2 after sorting: every permanent sup plus two rotating marks, prioritizing the current Мишень class and excluding the active Francie order. unknown_bug is excluded. A living Most-wanted Rick always occupies one of the two rotating marks, even while he is the active Francie order (`CP:6758-6765`). A resolved attack on a marked sup gives +10 Skill (+20 СуперМудень), win or loss; every actual Drop pays +1 regular (+2 under СуперМудень). Кочерга uses fight-Skill and Harm multiplier `1 + pokerCount`. The appended improvement text is exactly «При прокачке Бучер получает кочергу.» (`CharacterPassives` Butcher hooks; `DoomsdayMachine` Butcher Harm/Drop path).
- **Kimiko** — at level 0, defense ignores exactly **1** attacker Justice in that fight's fresh clone. A defensive loss arms the existing one-round disabled state; it activates next round and recovers on the following transition. Her +10 defense-win / +20 successful-block Skill remains base. **Регенирация** starts at x1: Kimiko can no longer be disabled, ordinary Harm is intercepted, and each defense steals attacker Justice before resolution—2 at x1, 3 at x2, 4 at x3, 5 at x4. Combat changes only the current fight clone; the actual removable amount is separately debited from the attacker's persistent Justice and credited to TheBoys, so another simultaneous fight still sees the frozen round-start value. unknown_bug rejects the theft (`CharacterPassives` Kimiko defense/after-fight/next-round cases; `CharacterClass.LowerQualityResist`).
- **Живое Оружие** (Kimiko x4) — the pre-fight Justice theft now also pays the same amount as **regular points**. Before any fights resolve, every non-Block/non-Skip target of TheBoys has both persistent and fight-snapshot passive lists cleared for the rest of the match; accumulated stats, score and state remain. A permanent marker also makes name-based Gordon/Naruto/Sakura/DooM helpers, Salldorum/Geralt readiness actions, the Goblin final Ziggurat enforcement and later special level-ups inert. unknown_bug is immune and loses neither passives nor Justice (`TheBoys.DisablePassivesBeforeFights`; `DoomsdayMachine.CalculateAllFights`; `GameReactions.GetLvlUp`; `CheckIfReady` marker guards).
- **M.M.** — base team psyche: +1 if zero losses this round, −1 + психует if every resolved fight was a loss. First Psyche upgrade immediately makes M.M. calm (immune to `MinusPsyche`) and arms Компромат. The next actual attacking fight files the target; round 8 all-correct files pay +5 Мораль each, and game-end correct-prediction bonus is ×kompromat count. **Оковы Правосудия** (x4) steals current Мораль from every filed target and blocks later Moral gains; unknown_bug is skipped before both sides of the transfer (`CharacterPassives` M.M. cases; `GameReactions.GetLvlUp`; `CheckIfReady.HandleLastRound`).
- **Убийственная Парочка** — exact first-four distribution Francie x2 + Kimiko x2. The hidden card is mechanically inert until `ActiveCombination` names it and that exact card has been revealed; merely carrying the initial `Visible=false` card cannot run the effect or expose its name in a personal log. In every resolving attack or defense fight, after both sides' ordinary before-fight modifiers, an enemy that is TooGood or TooStronk for TheBoys loses its remaining Justice before calculation. The current fight clones receive the full drain/credit used by combat math, while the lasting transfer separately moves only the amount still available in persistent Justice; neither write changes another fight's frozen template. Block/Skip no-fights and unknown_bug are excluded (`TheBoys.HasActiveCombination`/`ApplyKillingCoupleJustice`; `DoomsdayMachine.CalculateAllFights`; M147/M168).
- **Нахер Бучера** — the first four upgrades include Francie, Kimiko and M.M. at least once each and none in Butcher (therefore a 2/1/1 distribution). Butcher marks, hunt Skill, Кочерга and Drop payouts stop immediately; Butcher cannot be upgraded again. The shared Justice object receives a permanent floor of **1**, enforced by every real/one-fight set, add and round settlement (`GameReactions.ResolveTheBoysFourthUpgrade`; `JusticeClass.SetMinimumRealJustice`).
- **Неудержимые** — the first four upgrades are split only between Butcher and Kimiko, with both represented. The accumulated raw Skill at unlock is added again once, exactly doubling it before later gains (`GameReactions.ResolveTheBoysFourthUpgrade`).
- **Sausage Party** — the first four upgrades include Francie, Butcher and M.M. at least once each and none in Kimiko. Immediately grants +1 Int, +1 Str and +1 Psyche (`GameReactions.ResolveTheBoysFourthUpgrade`).
- **TheBoys** — exact 1/1/1/1 distribution. Every member counter gains one extra level without any of the four +2 stat upgrades and without replaying first-upgrade side effects. Its supplied description additionally says `Получают зарплату от провительства при победе в матче.` If this combination is still active and TheBoys is an authoritative match winner at actual place 1, final account settlement adds **69 ZBS points** with the separate personal receipt `От самого призедента` (`GameReactions.ResolveTheBoysFourthUpgrade`; `TheBoys.ShouldAwardGovernmentSalary`; `CheckIfReady.HandleLastRound`).
- **Смертельный вирус** (Francie x4): the next real attack infects and emits the Francie/Butcher lines together as one paired phrase; later real fights spread it (Block/Skip excluded; Francie immune). At game end every installed mark steals **3 bonus points** for its source, even if the infected seat or Francie died after infection; a dispersed Naruto clone's liability resolves against the original. The ordinary zero floor still lets a victim lose less than 3 while Francie receives the full nominal 3. unknown_bug cannot be infected directly or through spread, a direct attempt against it keeps the armed use, and СуперМудень still cancels the virus (`CharacterPassives` `Francie`/`TheBoysSpreadVirus`; `CheckIfReady.HandleLastRound`; `TheBoys.VirusPointsPerInfected`; M148/m56).
- **СуперМудень** (Butcher x4) — hides/locks every card except Butcher and СуперМудень, clears any displayed active combination and disables every remaining Francie/Kimiko/M.M./Убийственная Парочка path. Later level-up points are consumed without reinforcement. It doubles the complete Butcher results: Кочерга #4 fight Skill ×5→×10 and base Harm 5→10. Every applied Int/Str/Psyche pool break queues another recursive Harm; enemy Harm interceptors are bypassed, score-0 stops the chain and at most 50 actual Drops land per turn (`TheBoys.LockNonButcherPassives`; `GameReactions.GetLvlUp`; `DoomsdayMachine` Butcher Harm path).
- Bot boundary: fair L2/L3 choose one of the four focused ultimates or five combination build sequences. They do not inspect hidden target immunity/quality pools and value only own state, public 🦸 rows and evidence-based fight estimates (`BotsBehavior.EnsureBotPlaystyle` / `HandleLvlUpBot` / `ApplyFairCharacterPreference`). Legacy L1 retains its old exact-state Harm scorer.
- UI boundary: the owner alone sees 🦸 on Butcher-marked enemy rows and ☣️ on rows infected by that owner's virus (web `GameStateMapper.MapPlayer` / `PlayerCard.vue`; Discord `GameUpdateMess.CustomLeaderBoardAfterPlayer`). Affected targets, unrelated players, spectators and admins receive neither private marker; the owner widget may also list the infected names (m27/m55 fixed).

## Homelander — Tier 2, Int 5 / Str 10 / Speed 9 / Psyche 1

State lives in `Game/Characters/Homelander.cs`: persistent per-enemy rage marks, revealers, the all-correct-prediction release, one-use Milk, the once-per-match `StanEdgarResolved` latch, current laser target, whether the conditional Seven points were ever frozen, and independent shuffle-bag indices for every supplied phrase pool. Phrase text and player-facing passive descriptions are stored verbatim in `CharactersPhrases.cs` and `characters.json`.

Tier 2 gives a natural roll weight of 70. Bot seats can roll Tier 3 and above only, with Кира as the sole lower-tier exception, so Homelander remains human-only. The Discord **Auto Move** button is not offered because that button requires `Tier > 3` (`StartGameLogic.GetRangeFromTier`/`HandleCharacterRoll`; `GameUpdateMess` action-row builder).

- **Classifications** — Homelander belongs to the lore class Бог and is a permanent Super for every TheBoys marking path (`GodClass.IsGod`/`IsGodDeathSource`; `TheBoys.IsPermanentSup`).
- **Roster pull** (hidden, roll-time) — Homelander contributes the ordinary `×2` for being a canonical roster Super and then his separate `×6` (`+500%`) modifier, for a combined `×12` TheBoys weight. This composes with every other Super's doubling and applies to remaining natural rolls plus all draft alternatives; see TheBoys above (`StartGameLogic.TheBoysRollMultiplierPerSuper`/`HomelanderTheBoysRollMultiplier`; `HandleCharacterRoll`/`RollDraftOptions`; M186).
- **Праведность** — Homelander has a virtual +7 Seven score while he is the living place-1 player. It creates no score entry and therefore is not turn income for Vought; leaving first place freezes it, returning reactivates it and emits the private sourced line `Праведность: Сильная личность!`. His place is snapshotted at the start of the fight phase. If he was first, every main-fight victory and an isolated victory over Нечто stamps `LastResolvedWinRound`; after Нечто has resolved, a same-round stamp pays +7 Moral exactly once, while a winless turn pays nothing. The observer records only the round and therefore cannot duplicate the payout when Homelander wins several fights. While that top-1 snapshot is active, Праведность charges no rage at all: neither the ordinary leader charge nor rage from an enemy victory over Homelander. Otherwise the current enemy leader receives +25% persistent rage, and any enemy victory over Homelander adds +10%; if that enemy was the leader charged in the same turn, the victory adds +50% instead, for +75% total that turn. Teammate and unknown_bug triggers are excluded; rage caps at 100%. At 100%, Homelander's next attack against that enemy breaks Block/Skip and is a terminal automatic win, except that unknown_bug remains the final outcome authority. After ordinary attacker Harm, the laser grants Homelander +10 Moral once and adds two guaranteed Drops even at place 6 or score 0, then resets that enemy's rage to 0. The laser is **repeatable without limit against the same enemy**: spending it only empties the bar, so the same enemy can charge Homelander again and be lasered any number of times. An armed laser whose fight is instead decided by a higher terminal outcome (unknown_bug, round-7 Мадара, a 228-Skill Sirinoks Дракон) never reaches the drop/reset step, so that mark stays at 100% and can still fire later. The owner alone sees the red percentage bar beside each enemy; a laser fight carries a DTO flag for the full-screen `ЛАЗЕР ИЗ ГЛАЗ` presentation (`Homelander.ApplyRighteousness`/`RecordResolvedWin`/`SettleRighteousnessMoral`/`RecordEnemyVictory`/`ArmLaser`/`ApplyLaserDrops`/`UpdateSevenPointsAvailability`; `Cthulhu.ResolveNechtoAttacks`; `InGameStatusClass.GetScore`; `DoomsdayMachine.CalculateAllFights`).
- **Скромность** — when M.M., Толя, DeepList, Молодой Глеб's ward, Геральт's senses or Кира's eyes reveals Homelander, that revealer is stored once and Homelander's Justice is forced to 0 in every later fight with them. Homelander alone sees `👁️` beside every stored revealer in both leaderboards. After prediction locking, if all five enemies guessed Homelander exactly, this Justice suppression is cancelled for everyone. Reveal/all-correct messages are private to Homelander (`Homelander.RecordReveal`/`WasRevealedBy`/`SuppressJustice`/`EvaluatePredictions`; the six reveal call sites in `CharacterPassives`).
- **Башня Vought** — after all ordinary `HandleEndOfRound` passives but before round-score settlement, positive score earned this turn is projected with the current regular multiplier. If exactly one living player has the highest positive total and it is an enemy, that enemy receives −1 immediate bonus point from `Vought`; teammates are ignored. If the unique maximum is Homelander, every positive regular and bonus entry he earned this turn is added once more, exactly doubling that positive income. A tied maximum does nothing (`Homelander.ApplyVoughtTower`; `DoomsdayMachine.CalculateAllFights`).
- **Супер "Человек"** — receiver-side point, Moral, Skill, Psyche, stat and Justice losses, ordinary Harm and passive shutdown are rejected by the central mutators. A protected point-transfer attempt creates no paired thief credit; any pre-recorded deferred reclaim entry is discarded before settlement. Existing state/control caveats remain intentional: Ктулху may still impose the Морок psyche cap/mad state, Гордон may still establish zombie/headcrab state and its Intelligence cap, Толя may still set the round-multiplier override, and Еврей's direct victory-point redirect remains outside the receiver guard. The protection is disabled for every mutual fight with TheBoys and while TheBoys applies Компромат, Смертельный вирус, Оковы Правосудия or Живое Оружие; their ordinary effects then work unchanged. Живое Оружие now also makes all Homelander passive-keyed helpers inert, freezes the Seven score and removes private rage/revealer UI for the rest of the match; the hidden **Stan Edgar** audit is the one deliberate exception, because it is keyed on the character name alone and its 33-point debit is taken through `RunWithoutProtection`. The effect that Пацаны cause on Homelander remains permanent Super classification through `TheBoys.IsPermanentSup` (`Homelander.IsProtected`/`CanTransferFrom`/`RunWithoutProtection`/`OnPassivesDisabled`; `CharacterClass`; `InGameStatusClass`; `GamePlayerBridgeClass`; `TheBoys.DisablePassivesBeforeFights`).
- **Молоко** (hidden) — the first time Homelander submits an attack against HardKitty, he gains +1 regular point; fight outcome is irrelevant (`Homelander.TryMilk`).
- **Лидер Семерки** (hidden) — final game initialization moves Homelander to place 1 unless Злой Школьник is in the roster. If unknown_bug already occupies place 1, its position isolation is preserved and Homelander is inserted second (`Homelander.MoveToInitialLead`; `CharacterPassives.HandlePassiveAtGameStart`).
- **Stan Edgar** (hidden; deliberately absent from the player-facing description and from `characters.json`, it is an end-game score source only) — Vought audits turn 10. It runs exactly once, immediately after round-10 score settlement and Сайтама's round-11 reclaim, while the round counter has just reached 11. If Homelander's score is then **below 70** — `GetScore()`, so an active virtual Seven counts — one extra fight row visible to everyone is appended to the round-10 fight table, `Stan Edgar ⟶ Stan Edgar` against the Homelander player's nickname, backed by a synthetic public `FightEntryDto` with the Stan Edgar avatar in the same way Ктулху's Нечто row works. The visible log order is exactly the four global lines—threshold Stan, Homelander, product Stan, sourceless `Залп V-наводящихся ракет...`—followed by Homelander's additional private bilingual Нуар line; the client preserves this public-then-private sequence even though its ordinary event merge is personal-first (m69). Last, Homelander loses **33 bonus points** under the source `Stan Edgar`. Because the debit runs through `RunWithoutProtection` it defeats **Супер "Человек"**; the ordinary score floor at 0 still applies, and the loss lands before the leaderboard sort, so it decides his final place. Unlike the rest of the kit this is gated on the character name alone: Kimiko's **Живое Оружие** does not disable it and neither does death. It is cancelled only by an early game end — **Космический ужас**, Omni-man's invasion or **Вечное Цукуеми** all return before the call site (`Homelander.ApplyStanEdgar`; `DoomsdayMachine.CompleteRoundAsync`; `Madara.IsEternalTsukuyomiActive`).
- Supplied random lines use independent non-repeating shuffle bags; a pool refills only after every phrase in that category has appeared. Every emitted Homelander line is prefixed by its passive source, and interpolated enemy nicknames are bold (`Homelander.Take`; `CharactersUniquePhrase` Homelander lists).

## Omni-man — Tier 2, Int 2 / Str 10 / Speed 10 / Psyche 6

State lives in `Game/Characters/OmniMan.cs`: the per-enemy idiot set, strict dialogue indices, non-repeating phrase bags, delayed-invasion round/serial, **Стражи Земли** sleep projection and the one-shot **Подземный Поезд** state/serial/phrase. All supplied descriptions and phrases remain verbatim in `characters.json` and the Omni-man lists in `CharactersPhrases.cs`.

- **Classifications** — Omni-man is a permanent Super for every TheBoys marking path. All four passives are owner-bound (`Standalone: false`) and every mechanic validates the real `Name == "Omni-man"` holder (`TheBoys.Superheroes`; `OmniMan.Is`/`HasPassive`).
- **Подумай, Марк!** — after every resolved attacking win, compare the target's current fight Intelligence with Omni-man's. The target resists only at Omni-man Intelligence +2 or higher; every resisted check advances the supplied ten-line sequence. A failed target is added to the private idiot set once and advances the supplied five-dialogue sequence. The first conversion gives Omni-man +5 immediate bonus points from exact source `Битва интеллекта`; each already-converted idiot reduces the next payout by 1, with a minimum of 1, so the five possible enemy payouts are 5/4/3/2/1. Later wins still check Intelligence but cannot mark or pay for that target again. The enemy nickname that sources the reply is bold. The Omni-man owner alone sees `🤡` on each marked enemy in both web and Discord leaderboards (`OmniMan.HandleIntelligenceWin`; `CharacterPassives.HandleAttackAfterFight`; `GameStateMapper.MapPlayer`; `GameUpdateMess.CustomLeaderBoardAfterPlayer`).
- **Вилтрумайтское вторжение** — after ordinary end-of-round passives, if every remaining eligible enemy is in the idiot set, invasion is scheduled for the end of the following turn. Dead players, teammates, unknown_bug, sealed Мадара, dispersed Naruto clones and the shared round-10 ban are excluded. Scheduling is disabled on turn 10, so the last legal schedule is turn 9 and the last legal trigger is the end of turn 10. At the trigger the exact global announcement is emitted, `InvasionSerial` increments, Omni-man is forced to place 1 and `IsFinished` stops the game on that round. Final settlement bypasses predictions and every other ordinary end-game winner modifier, ignores team totals and score ties, and awards sole first-place history/mastery/ZBS/box/achievement treatment to Omni-man. **Космический ужас** has unconditional priority: if it has fired, a pending invasion is cancelled and cannot supply the winner even from stale dual state (M154; `OmniMan.EvaluateInvasion`/`TryTriggerInvasion`/`GetInvasionWinner`/`ForceFirstPlace`; `DoomsdayMachine.CompleteRoundAsync`; `CheckIfReady.HandleLastRound`).
- **Стражи Земли** — before Homelander's **Башня Vought**, project every living enemy's net round delta: bonus entries stay ×1, while positive and negative regular entries use that player's current round multiplier. Exactly one strictly positive maximum receives the existing `GlebTeaTriggeredWhen` sleep on the next turn and therefore the unchanged target log `Тебя усыпили...`; ties/non-positive turns do nothing, and neither Gleb nor Omni-man receives score. Omni-man's private supplied line carries exact source `Стражи Земли:`, bolds every occurrence of the enemy nickname and advances by successful trigger, with the ninth line reused only if further triggers were possible. During the affected turn only Omni-man sees `😴` beside that enemy in web and Discord; the marker requires an actually active Skip, so Portal Gun prevention or Premade cancellation removes it. No sleep is scheduled after turn 9 (M155/m59; `OmniMan.ApplyGuardiansOfTheGlobe`/`IsSleepingFromGuardians`; `DoomsdayMachine.CalculateAllFights`; `CharacterPassives.HandleNextRound`; `GameStateMapper.MapPlayer`; `GameUpdateMess.CustomLeaderBoardAfterPlayer`).
- **Частица нашей силы** — every enemy attack submitted against a living Omni-man grants that attacker +20 persistent Skill before Block/Skip resolution. Each attempt also gives Omni-man exactly one private phrase with source `Частица нашей силы:`, drawn from a 17-entry shuffle bag; the bag refills only after all entries have appeared, so multiple incoming attacks in one turn each pay and consume a phrase independently (`OmniMan.HandleEnemyAttack`; `CharacterPassives.HandleDefenseBeforeFight`).
- **Подземный Поезд** — match-once, after a resolved attack win only when Omni-man entered that fight at place 1 and the defeated enemy at place 2. Four ordinary Drops are attempted against the target with the place-6/zero-score gates lifted; normal unknown_bug/Homelander Drop protection and the end-of-round Ziggurat/HardKitty movement rules remain authoritative. The number of successful Drops defines how many immediately lower pre-Drop rows form the path. Every living non-team enemy on those crossed rows, except unknown_bug or a protected Homelander, loses `ceil(current score × 20%)` immediate bonus points and `ceil(current Moral × 20%)` Moral, each with a minimum of 1; Omni-man receives both nominal amounts. The ordinary score-zero-floor/D12 nominal-credit rule remains, while Jon's royal bonus path is normalized so the score debit is still exactly the calculated 20%. Moral changes pass through the central `AddMoral` interceptors, and the helper compensates for that mutator's internal ×2 storage so the visible debit/credit equals the calculated amount. The use is consumed once the trigger conditions match. Omni-man receives one random supplied phrase and an owner-only 5.4-second full-screen, blood-stained Invincible-style train overlay; reconnects do not replay an old serial. ## Джон Сноу — Tier 5, Int 1 / Str 4 / Speed 5 / Psyche 8

All mechanics and match state live in `Game/Characters/JonSnow.cs`; the six passives are non-Standalone, the complete definition is excluded from ARAM and all six are filtered out of Бензопила choices (`CharactersPull.GetAramPassives`; CP Бензопила choice filter). - **Тупой бастард** — every positive Мишень or extra-Skill gain is doubled. On each resolved Jon victory, the same Step-1 difficulty classification used for **Я Джон Сноу** grants +1 persistent Intelligence for `toogood` or +2 for `toostronk`; the exact amount actually added is tracked.
