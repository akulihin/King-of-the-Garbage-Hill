## TheBoys (Пацаны) — Tier 3, Int 3 / Str 5 / Speed 6 / Psyche 2

State classes `FrancieClass`, `ButcherClass`, `KimikoClass`, and `MMClass` live in `Game/Characters/TheBoys.cs`; `ButcherClass` also records whether the fourth team upgrade was resolved, the active combination and whether Butcher left. Permanent sups = Сайтама, Кратос, Загадочный Спартанец в маске, Кира, Молодой Глеб, and old Глеб only during his Претендент round (`TheBoys.IsPermanentSup`); two more rotate. Per-player marks: `TheBoysSupMark`, `TheBoysVirus(+Source)`, `TheBoysMoralBlockedByMM`, plus the permanent `PassiveAbilitiesDisabledByKimiko` marker. UI serials distinguish member reveals, ultimate unlocks and lower-intensity combination unlocks.

- **Team upgrade** — there is no separate `Пацаны` passive card. The four always-visible member cards are Francie, Butcher, Kimiko and M.M. A level-up gives **+2** to the chosen stat and levels the matching member: Int→Francie, Str→Butcher, Speed→Kimiko, Psyche→M.M. The first member upgrade appends the supplied improvement text; x4 of one member unlocks its ultimate. Exactly after the fourth distributed team upgrade, `ResolveTheBoysFourthUpgrade` checks one secret combination and never re-evaluates later distributions (`GameReactions.GetLvlUp` / `ResolveTheBoysFourthUpgrade`).
- **Francie** — first order at game start, replacements on rounds 4 and 7, 3-turn windows. Completion buffers **+1 regular point** and expiry on rounds 4/7/10 buffers **−1 regular point**, so both use the current round multiplier. A final-turn win clears `OrderTarget` in the per-fight hook before expiry. Хим.оружие (level ≥1) still pays `chemLevel × (1 + enemy TooGood + enemy TooStronk)` **bonus** points on an eligible win (`CharacterPassives` Francie start/after-fight/next-round cases; m10 fixed).
- **Butcher** — marks begin on round 2 after sorting: every permanent sup plus two rotating marks, prioritizing the current Мишень class and excluding the active Francie order. unknown_bug is excluded. A resolved attack on a marked sup gives +10 Skill (+20 СуперМудень), win or loss; every actual Drop pays +1 regular (+2 under СуперМудень). Кочерга uses fight-Skill and Harm multiplier `1 + pokerCount`. The appended improvement text is exactly «При прокачке Бучер получает кочергу.» (`CharacterPassives` Butcher hooks; `DoomsdayMachine` Butcher Harm/Drop path).
- **Kimiko** — at level 0, defense ignores exactly **1** attacker Justice for that fight. A defensive loss arms the existing one-round disabled state; it activates next round and recovers on the following transition. Her +10 defense-win / +20 successful-block Skill remains base. **Регенирация** starts at x1: Kimiko can no longer be disabled, ordinary Harm is intercepted, and each defense steals real attacker Justice before resolution—2 at x1, 3 at x2, 4 at x3, 5 at x4—transferring the actual amount to TheBoys. unknown_bug rejects the theft (`CharacterPassives` Kimiko defense/after-fight/next-round cases; `CharacterClass.LowerQualityResist`).
- **Живое Оружие** (Kimiko x4) — the pre-fight Justice theft now also pays the same amount as **regular points**. Before any fights resolve, every non-Block/non-Skip target of TheBoys has both persistent and fight-snapshot passive lists cleared for the rest of the match; accumulated stats, score and state remain. A permanent marker also makes name-based Gordon/Naruto/Sakura/DooM helpers, Salldorum/Geralt readiness actions, the Goblin final Ziggurat enforcement and later special level-ups inert. unknown_bug is immune and loses neither passives nor Justice (`TheBoys.DisablePassivesBeforeFights`; `DoomsdayMachine.CalculateAllFights`; `GameReactions.GetLvlUp`; `CheckIfReady` marker guards).
- **M.M.** — base team psyche: +1 if zero losses this round, −1 + психует if every resolved fight was a loss. First Psyche upgrade immediately makes M.M. calm (immune to `MinusPsycheLog`) and arms Компромат. The next actual attacking fight files the target; round 8 all-correct files pay +5 Мораль each, and game-end correct-prediction bonus is ×kompromat count. **Оковы Правосудия** (x4) steals current Мораль from every filed target and blocks later Moral gains; unknown_bug is skipped before both sides of the transfer (`CharacterPassives` M.M. cases; `GameReactions.GetLvlUp`; `CheckIfReady.HandleLastRound`).
- **Убийственная Парочка** — exact first-four distribution Francie x2 + Kimiko x2. In every resolving attack or defense fight, after both sides' ordinary before-fight modifiers, an enemy that is TooGood or TooStronk for TheBoys loses its remaining real Justice before calculation; that amount is added to TheBoys. Block/Skip no-fights and unknown_bug are excluded (`TheBoys.ApplyKillingCoupleJustice`; `DoomsdayMachine.CalculateAllFights`).
- **Нахер Бучера** — the first four upgrades include Francie, Kimiko and M.M. at least once each and none in Butcher (therefore a 2/1/1 distribution). Butcher marks, hunt Skill, Кочерга and Drop payouts stop immediately; Butcher cannot be upgraded again. The shared Justice object receives a permanent floor of **1**, enforced by every real/one-fight set, add and round settlement (`GameReactions.ResolveTheBoysFourthUpgrade`; `JusticeClass.SetMinimumRealJustice`).
- **Неудержимые** — the first four upgrades are split only between Butcher and Kimiko, with both represented. The accumulated raw Skill at unlock is added again once, exactly doubling it before later gains (`GameReactions.ResolveTheBoysFourthUpgrade`).
- **Sausage Party** — the first four upgrades include Francie, Butcher and M.M. at least once each and none in Kimiko. Immediately grants +1 Int, +1 Str and +1 Psyche (`GameReactions.ResolveTheBoysFourthUpgrade`).
- **TheBoys** — exact 1/1/1/1 distribution. Every member counter gains one extra level without any of the four +2 stat upgrades and without replaying first-upgrade side effects. Its supplied description additionally says `Получают зарплату от провительства при победе в матче.` If this combination is still active and TheBoys is an authoritative match winner at actual place 1, final account settlement adds **69 ZBS points** with the separate personal receipt `От самого призедента` (`GameReactions.ResolveTheBoysFourthUpgrade`; `TheBoys.ShouldAwardGovernmentSalary`; `CheckIfReady.HandleLastRound`).
- **Смертельный вирус** (Francie x4): next real attack infects; spreads on later real fights (block/skip excluded; Francie immune); game end steals 2 bonus per infected (`CP:2401-2438,3441-3460`, `CheckIfReady.cs:367-392`). unknown_bug cannot be infected directly or through spread; a direct attempt keeps the armed use, and stale infected/score-successor state creates no final debit or Francie credit.
- **СуперМудень** (Butcher x4) — hides/locks every card except Butcher and СуперМудень, clears any displayed active combination and disables every remaining Francie/Kimiko/M.M./Убийственная Парочка path. Later level-up points are consumed without reinforcement. It doubles the complete Butcher results: Кочерга #4 fight Skill ×5→×10 and base Harm 5→10. Every applied Int/Str/Psyche pool break queues another recursive Harm; enemy Harm interceptors are bypassed, score-0 stops the chain and at most 50 actual Drops land per turn (`TheBoys.LockNonButcherPassives`; `GameReactions.GetLvlUp`; `DoomsdayMachine` Butcher Harm path).
- Bot boundary: fair L2/L3 choose one of the four focused ultimates or five combination build sequences. They do not inspect hidden target immunity/quality pools and value only own state, public 🦸 rows and evidence-based fight estimates (`BotsBehavior.EnsureBotPlaystyle` / `HandleLvlUpBot` / `ApplyFairCharacterPreference`). Legacy L1 retains its old exact-state Harm scorer.
- UI boundary: the owner alone sees 🦸 on marked enemy rows (web `GameStateMapper.cs:137-145,345-351` / `PlayerCard.vue:738-741`; Discord `GameUpdateMess.cs:697-719`). Targets receive no sup notification and the owner widget no longer lists their nicknames (m27 fixed).

## Сайтама — Tier 5, Int 1 / Str 10 / Speed 10 / Psyche 10

State: `Saitama.cs` — deferral ledger (round-multiplied), deferred moral, 2 "serious targets".

- **Лысина** — +1000 Skill at game start (`CP:252-255`).
- **Неприметность** — serious targets = top-2 enemies by `GetSkill()`, recomputed every round end (`CP:4417-4425`; round 1 uses game-start skills ⚠ effectively arbitrary). Rounds 1–9, defending vs a non-serious attacker: Saitama can't win; the foregone win point (round-multiplied) + underdog moral are **banked**, no upfront loss, and the staged loss grants Saitama **no Justice** (`CP:667-690`; `DM:1022-1023,1138-1140`). Attacking a target that a co-attacker also hit: his +1 is deferred to the recipient (−1 regular now, banked) along with moral (`CP:2167-2200`). Solo wins vs Мишень-class: см. На мели.
- **На мели** — Saitama gets no regular victory point when he wins as the defender; victory points are paid only when he wins as the attacker (`DM:1160-1176`). On an attacking win vs his Мишень-class target: +1 bonus; if nobody else attacked the victim: +1 regular and the fight is hidden from non-admin logs (`CP:2137-2165`).
- **Ищет достойного противника** — after round 10 (`RoundNo == 11`, `CP:4827-4876`): if Saitama's round-10 attack beat the then-#1, he reclaims the whole ledger (+bonus to himself, −banked amount from each recipient), restores deferred moral and force-converts it to score with a manual flush. A point that would belong to unknown_bug is never entered into the ledger (and cannot be rerouted to a Jew), so reclaim creates neither a debit nor Saitama credit for it. On round 10 Неприметность is off (`CP:610, 2001`), so nothing new defers.

## Тигр — Tier 5, Int 1 / Str 9 / Speed 9 / Psyche 1

State: `Tigr.cs` (`TigrTopClass.TimeCount = 3`, three-zero series list).

- **Лучше с двумя, чем с адекватными** — end of each round: +3 bonus per unique player whose Int **or** Psyche equals Тигр's (`CP:3454-3470`). The loop counts Тигр himself, so he always gets a free +3 at the end of round 1 — **intended** (Тигр is a member of his own clan; M6, ОК).
- **3-0 обоссан** — per-enemy win series; 3 consecutive wins (loss resets, simultaneous win+loss at ≥2 keeps the series): +3 regular, +30 Skill, +3 Мораль; victim −1 Int −1 Psyche (MinusPsycheLog); once per enemy (`CP:3767-3835`).
- **Тигр топ, а ты холоп** — while `TimeCount > 0`, end-of-round swap into place 1 (Ziggurat-protected, consumes 1 count; suppressed on round 10 if banned) (`DoomsdayMachine.cs:1384-1412`). unknown_bug already in place 1 cannot be displaced at either the opening or end-of-round activation, and the blocked activation does not consume a count. Random re-arm: on a `TigrTopWhen` round (1–8, 1–2 times) the counter resets to 3 (`CP:4974-4978`). Additionally fires **at game start**: `HandleEventsBeforeFirstRound` (`CP:212-225`) swaps him to place 1 and consumes a count from the initial 3 — an opening window (rounds 1–3), confirmed **intended** in addition to the random window (M5, ОК). Being #1 on rounds 2–9 (however achieved): +1 Psyche +3 Мораль (`CP:5960-5967`).
