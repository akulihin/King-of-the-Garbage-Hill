### Achievement V2 overlay

Every one of the 40 character definitions/forms has a paired achievement story: one **normal** requirement and one **hard** requirement, for 80 character achievements total. Difficulty and reward rarity are separate labels: the three pre-existing hard cards for Рик Санчез, Итачи and Котики remain Rare so already-issued rewards do not change. This includes Молодой Глеб as the `Main Ирелия` form of the `Глеб` runtime name, the original Наруто rather than his clones, and native Братишка rather than mylorik's transformed copy. Thirteen additional secret achievements observe specific character pairings (cross-reference [INTERACTION-MATRIX.md](INTERACTION-MATRIX.md) §8). These hooks do not change the passive rules documented below; they only record match-local facts and evaluate them at game end (`AchievementClass.cs` `AllAchievements`/`TrackGameEnd`). Exact requirements, rewards, secret/privacy behavior and compatibility with the original V2 catalog are in [ACHIEVEMENTS.md](ACHIEVEMENTS.md).

## TheBoys (Пацаны) — Tier 3, Int 3 / Str 5 / Speed 6 / Psyche 2

State classes `FrancieClass`, `ButcherClass`, `KimikoClass`, and `MMClass` live in `Game/Characters/TheBoys.cs:46-94`. Permanent sups = Сайтама, Кратос, Загадочный Спартанец в маске, Кира, Молодой Глеб, and old Глеб only during his Претендент round (`TheBoys.cs:31-44`); two more rotate. Per-player marks: `TheBoysSupMark`, `TheBoysVirus(+Source)`, `TheBoysMoralBlockedByMM`. UI serials drive reveal/unlock animations.

- **Пацаны** — level-up gives **+2** to the chosen stat and levels the matching member: Int→Francie, Str→Butcher, Speed→Kimiko, Psyche→M.M. (`GameReactions.cs:975-1064`). First upgrade appends the supplied upgrade line to that member's description; 4th unlocks the ultimate. Once СуперМудень is active, later level-up points are consumed without reaching the other members (`GameReactions.cs:976-981`).
- **Francie** — first order at game start (`CP:368-384`), replacements on rounds 4 and 7, 3-turn windows; completion = +1 bonus, expiry (rounds 4/7/10) = −1 bonus (`CP:3549-3567,6110-6140`). A final-turn win is settled in the per-fight hook and clears `OrderTarget` **before** the round-transition expiry/new-target check; the display counter cannot veto it (`CP:3554-3567,6114-6135`). Хим.оружие (level ≥1): eligible win pays **`chemLevel × difficulty`** bonus, difficulty = 1 + enemy-TooGood + enemy-TooStronk (`CP:3569-3583`; m10 fixed).
- **Butcher** — marks begin on round 2 after sorting: all permanent sups +2 rotating marks, prioritizing current Мишень class and excluding the active Francie order while Francie exists (`CP:6273-6307`). A resolved attack on a marked sup gives +10 Skill (+20 СуперМудень), win or loss (`CP:3536-3555`). Every actual Drop of that sup gives **+1 regular point** (therefore round-multiplied), or **+2 regular per Drop** under СуперМудень (`DoomsdayMachine.cs:1036-1046`; M7 corrected). Кочерга uses fight-Skill multiplier `1 + pokerCount` and repeats Harm `1 + butcherState.PokerCount` times (`CP:1626-1636`, `DoomsdayMachine.cs:963-998`).
- **Kimiko** — her base +10 defense-win / +20 successful-block Skill and Harm guard exist at level 0 (`CP:853-875`, `CharacterClass.cs:217-228`). **Regeneration does not exist mechanically before x1**: Regen 0 cannot arm `DisabledNextRound`, become disabled, recover, or emit those phrases (`CP:1012-1022,6143-6168`). From x1, defense ignores up to RegenLevel attacker Justice for that fight; a defensive loss disables regeneration/guard for the next round, then it recovers (`CP:758-777,1012-1022,6143-6168`). **Живое Оружие** (x4): never disabled; after every defensive fight/block it drains the attacker's real Justice, transfers it to TheBoys, and grants the same number of **regular points** (`CP:853-875`).
- **M.M.** — base team psyche: +1 if zero losses this round, −1 + психует if every resolved fight was a loss (`CP:3684-3726`). **First Psyche upgrade immediately makes M.M. calm** (immune to all `MinusPsycheLog`) and arms Компромат (`GameReactions.cs:1028-1036`, `GamePlayerBridgeClass.cs:102-116`); calmness suppresses only the −1/psych-out, while the zero-loss +1 team Psyche continues. The next actual attacking fight files the target with one of the supplied character/position-specific hints (`CP:3558-3586,7176-7308`); round 8 all-correct files pay +5 Мораль each, and game-end correct-prediction bonus is ×kompromat count (`CP:3703-3717`, `CheckIfReady.cs:326-357`). **Оковы Правосудия** (x4) steals current Мораль from every filed target and blocks their later Moral gains; calmness is no longer part of this ultimate (`GameReactions.cs:1037-1057`).
- **Смертельный вирус** (Francie x4): next real attack infects; spreads on later real fights (block/skip excluded; Francie immune); game end steals 2 bonus per infected (`CP:2399-2436,3441-3460`, `CheckIfReady.cs:360-383`).
- **СуперМудень** (Butcher x4) — disables **every** remaining Francie/Kimiko/M.M. path, including seeded-virus spread/end payout, final компромат, calm immunity, recovery phrases and the stale order exclusion (`CP:2399-2436,3398-3403,3488-3492,3616-3649,5914-5972`; `CheckIfReady.cs:326-383`; `GamePlayerBridgeClass.cs:102-116`). It doubles the complete Butcher results: Кочерга #4 fight Skill ×5→×10 and base Harm 5→10 (`CP:1626-1636`, `DoomsdayMachine.cs:963-998`). Every Int/Str/Psyche pool break that actually applies its negative effect queues another Harm; those Harms recurse. It bypasses enemy Harm interceptors (including Madara, Boole Family, Kimiko and Испанец), allows last-place score Drops, stops at victim score 0, and caps at **50 actual Drops shared across the turn** before resetting next turn (`CharacterClass.cs:182-345`; `DoomsdayMachine.cs:973-1018`; `CP:6273-6276`; M17 fixed).
- Bots/Discord/web: bots understand the immunity bypass/full doubled Harm (`BotsBehavior.cs:1047-1073`); the owner alone sees 🦸 on marked enemy rows (web `GameStateMapper.cs:137-145,345-351` / `PlayerCard.vue:738-741`; Discord `GameUpdateMess.cs:697-719`). Targets receive no sup notification and the owner widget no longer lists their nicknames (m27 fixed).

Standing on any built position: position locked for the round (immune to Тигр-топ/Portal-Gun/Шэн/Drops/Storm — enforced in `DoomsdayMachine.cs:1332-1402`) +1 next-round Justice +5 Мораль per round. **Round-10 Ziggurat at place 1 = a real enforced win** in `HandleLastRound` (bonus-point overtake mirroring Premade; M1 fixed).
- Kill-immune (Кира `CP:4775`, Кратос `CP:1725`) — **except** Монстр's "Пейзаж конца света", which **does** kill goblin pawns on round 10 (the one kill-source they aren't immune to; M12, intended). Custom level-ups (rates/festival, `GameReactions.cs:786-840`).
